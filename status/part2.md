### P4 (Roselyn): Recovery Navigator & Infra
- **Presign and media routes** connect with a server-only **`STDB_WEB_TOKEN`** (its own identity; send the hex to Sidd to register as `web`) and subscribe to:
  - `web_report_upload_auth`: `{reportId, reporterSecretHash, status}` for uploadable reports. Media presign is allowed iff the row exists and `sha256(reporterSecret) === reporterSecretHash`.
  - `web_verification_auth`: `{verificationId, secretHash, state, propertyId}` for pending and verified verifications. idv presign needs `state==='pending'` and a matching hash. Media GET needs `state==='verified'` and a matching hash.
